History of Paris

Paris, the capital of France, has a rich and fascinating history spanning over 2,000 years. Founded by Celtic Gallic tribes, the city was later occupied by the Romans who called it "Lutetia." Over centuries, it has evolved into one of the world's most influential cities, shaping art, culture, philosophy, and science.

The medieval period saw the construction of Notre-Dame Cathedral, while the Renaissance brought artistic flourishing. The French Revolution in 1789 marked a turning point in world history, originating from the streets of Paris.

Art & Culture

Paris is the epitome of cultural sophistication. The city is home to world-class museums like the Louvre, which houses the Mona Lisa and attracts millions of visitors annually. The Musée d'Orsay showcases Impressionist masterpieces, while the Centre Pompidou displays contemporary art.

The city's neighborhoods each tell a unique story: Montmartre with its bohemian charm, Le Marais with its historic architecture, and the Latin Quarter with its intellectual heritage. Paris continues to inspire artists, writers, and creators from around the world.

French Cuisine & Dining

No visit to Paris is complete without experiencing its legendary culinary scene. From Michelin-starred restaurants to charming cafés, Paris offers gastronomic delights that cater to every palate. Iconic dishes include croissants, macarons, escargot, coq au vin, and exquisite wines from nearby regions.

The city's café culture is legendary. Sitting at a sidewalk café with a café au lait and a croissant, people-watching and soaking in the Parisian atmosphere, is an essential experience.
