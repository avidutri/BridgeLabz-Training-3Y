The shape of a sentence

Typography gives an idea its pace, voice, and room to breathe.

Good type is both clear and expressive. A readable page uses a considered font family, a comfortable line-height, and enough space between letters to keep every phrase open.

A serif face can feel literary, while a clean sans-serif voice feels direct. Contrast makes hierarchy visible. Use it with care, and a short article can feel composed rather than crowded.

Small choices accumulate. Weight, size, alignment, and rhythm all help a reader understand where to pause and what deserves attention.
