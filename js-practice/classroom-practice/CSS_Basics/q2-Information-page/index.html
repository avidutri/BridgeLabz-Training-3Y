The Pink City

Jaipure is one of India's most beautiful historic cities, famous for its architecture, fort and colorful culture.

History of Jaipure

Jaipur was found in 1727 by Maharaja sawai jai singh II. Thr city is known for its planned streets and magnificent historical architecture.

Culture and Food

Jaipur is famous for Rajasthani music, traditional clothing, handicrafts and delicious food such as dal baati churma.
